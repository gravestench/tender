# Provider capability and qualification matrix

Tender's implemented reference market is a US merchant charging USD. The Go adapters and embeddable simulator have **MOCK** coverage. No merchant credential, genuine processor transaction, approved live account, or production pricing is available in this repository. `SANDBOX` and `LIVE` qualification is **BLOCKED** for every rail. An adapter's Go capability describes a protocol operation; a host must also check its merchant entitlement, browser policy, and the evidence below before exposing a method.

The Adyen ACH route can reach eligible personal accounts at US banks and credit unions through the ACH network. It does not certify every bank or account and does not eliminate the originating processor or bank relationship. Tender has no validated processor fee comparison. Blacktop's existing multi-merchant Stripe Connect and payout flows are outside this single-merchant direct-acquiring slice. [ACH decision](adr/0005-us-ach-partner.md) · [Blacktop integration seam](blacktop-integration.md).

Eligibility further depends on the merchant account, funding source, region, currency, device, browser and authentication flow. Hosts must disable a route when any required condition or evidence is missing. MOCK outcomes never qualify a SANDBOX or LIVE route. Each provider guide states the exact configuration and unsupported operations.

## Release gates

G1 requires a tagged independent import and complete setup/migration documentation. G2 requires green unit, race, contract and offline browser suites with no unresolved critical security or financial defects. G3 requires actual provider sandbox evidence for every advertised rail. G4 requires observed on-page behavior on supported devices and a documented sensitive-data boundary. The [release evidence ledger](evidence/release-gates.md) records current results. Full-scope `v1.0.0` stays untagged while G3 and G4 are blocked. Production activation is a separate merchant decision after a controlled live pilot.

For each external scenario, record provider and API version, commit, environment, UTC time, region/currency, expected and observed result, sanitized transaction/event IDs and redacted browser or portal proof. Mark **PASS**, **FAIL**, **BLOCKED**, or **NOT APPLICABLE** with a reason. Keep synthetic evidence separate from provider records and keep secrets, bank numbers and card data out of artifacts.
